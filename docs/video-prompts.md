# Video prompts: every place in the game that wants motion

Generated by `npm run video:prompts`. Do not hand-edit.

**72 clips.** Every prompt carries the same STYLE anchor as the card
art, because motion that does not match the cards reads as glued on.

## Before generating anything

**Composite mode matters more than the prompt.** Veo will not give you an
alpha channel, so anything that sits *over* the board has to be generated on
pure black and composited with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, which drops the black
and keeps the light. That is why every fire, lightning and light effect below
says "on black". Darkening effects (ash, withering, scorch) are the reverse:
generate on white, composite with `multiply`.

| comp | how it is used | generate on |
|---|---|---|
| `background` | full-bleed behind a screen | any ground |
| `screen` | glow/fire/light over the board | **pure black** |
| `multiply` | ash/shadow/scorch over the board | **white** |
| `card` | inside a card frame | any ground |

**Loops will not loop.** Veo does not produce seamless loops. For the six
ambient clips, pick slow drifting motion where the first and last frames are
similar, then run two `<video>` elements offset by half the duration and
crossfade. A hard cut is visible on fast motion and invisible on drifting dust.

**Sizes.** The PWA precaches what it ships, so keep the whole video set under
roughly 15 MB. Ambient at 720p, overlays at 480p, beats at 360p. WebM/VP9 first
with an MP4/H.264 fallback for Safari. Short beats can be 12 fps without
anyone noticing, and hand-painted animation looks *better* at a lower rate.

**Portrait.** The game is mobile-first. The six ambient backgrounds want a 9:16
variant as well as 16:9; everything else can be centre-safe 16:9.

**Order of work.** If credits run short, tiers 1 and 2 alone (20 clips) carry
the whole feel of the game. Tier 5 is the least missed.
